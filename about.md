
# Welcome to the UOW Tree Walk

Explore the campus one tree at a time.

This self-guided walking tour helps you discover notable trees around UOW, follow nearby paths, and learn more about the landscape as you move through campus.

Use the map to select a tree, follow the suggested route, and enjoy the walk at your own pace.

**Tip:** Enable GPS to see your distance to selected trees and get route guidance from your current location.

**Start walking when you’re ready.**
